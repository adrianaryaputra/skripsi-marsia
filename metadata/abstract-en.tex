\abstrakinggris{This study discusses the marketing strategy of Nasi Gerilya Restaurant on the GrabFood platform to increase sales based on SWOT analysis. The study is motivated by unstable sales performance, a declining proportion of repeat orders, customer complaints, and competitive pressure from similar Padang food merchants on GrabFood. This research uses a descriptive qualitative approach with data collected through interviews, GrabFood platform observation, business documentation, customer reviews, and literature study. The analysis identifies internal and external factors, arranges strategic factors into IFAS and EFAS, and formulates alternative strategies through the SWOT matrix. This study is expected to produce relevant marketing strategy recommendations to strengthen competitiveness, customer experience, repeat purchases, and sales improvement opportunities for Nasi Gerilya Restaurant on the GrabFood platform.}
